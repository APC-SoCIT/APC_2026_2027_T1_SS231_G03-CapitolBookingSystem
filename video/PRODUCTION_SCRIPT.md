## Creative approach

The film is built as a three-act infomercial:

1. **Heritage and pain (0:00–0:29).** It opens on 86 years of Capitol celebrations, then cuts to the chaos behind them: phone calls, buried Messenger threads, handwritten logbooks and a double booking.
2. **The guest's journey (0:29–2:42).** The reveal, then one customer, Maria Santos, uses every customer feature in one continuous story: sign in, catering, the live calendar, function rooms, delivery, QR payment and tracking.
3. **Behind the counter (2:42–4:33).** The same day as seen by Capitol's team: the AI assistant, Inquiry Bot, Operations, delivery and menu management, the rider, analytics and roles, then the brand close.

The continuity is deliberate. Maria's order **CAP-1050** is paid by QR in scene 9, tracked in scene 10, assigned to a rider in scene 14 and delivered in scene 15. Ana Dizon's Messenger inquiry in scene 11 is answered in scene 12 and confirmed in scene 13. Viewers see one connected system, not a set of separate screens.

The motion language follows the brand: devices rise in with a soft 3D tilt, the cursor moves with ease-in-out and shows a click ripple, and totals count up rather than jump. Captions are burned in so the film works in a classroom with poor speakers.

## What is staged for the film

- **QR Ph payment** (scene 9) is a designed screen. The current code offers Cash on delivery, GCash and Card in a select, with no payment processing. The film shows the intended QR flow. The QR code is real but only encodes the text "CAPITOL BOOKING SYSTEM DEMO | Order CAP-1050 | PHP 2,220.00".
- **Food and venue photos** replace the app's placeholders. They are Wikimedia Commons photos, not photos of Capitol's own dishes or rooms (see `CREDITS.md`). Swap in Capitol's real photos when you have them.
- **Booking references** are shown as short codes (BK-7F3A21). The database stores UUIDs.
- **Role navigation** follows the team's intended matrix: Staff gets Operations, Delivery and Menu Items; Manager gets Operations, Menu Items and Dashboard; Admin gets everything. `src/lib/roles.ts` currently also gives Staff the Inquiry Bot.
- All names, phone numbers, orders and figures are fictional demo data. The dashboard numbers come from `public/analytic_dashboard/mock_data.json`.

## Editing and re-rendering

All sources are in `video/`. Preview by opening `index.html` in Chrome: Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys scrub, and `?t=95` jumps to a timecode.

1. Change any narration line in `script.json`, then run `python tts.py`. Visuals re-time automatically because every cue is anchored to spoken words.
2. Run `python meta.py`, then `python mix.py` to rebuild the music, sound effects and narration mix.
3. Run `python render.py --workers 6 --out out/Capitol_Booking_System_Demo.mp4`. Add `--cc 0` for a version without captions.

Requirements: Python with `edge-tts`, `playwright`, `imageio-ffmpeg`, `numpy`, `pillow` and `requests`, plus Google Chrome.
